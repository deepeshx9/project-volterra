\# Day 1 – Research Initialization



\## Project (DD/MM/YYYY is 04/05/2026)

AI-driven smart home autonomy system (experimental testbed)



\## Research Question

How does increasing autonomy affect:

\- error rate

\- user intervention

\- task success



\## Hypothesis

Moderate autonomy improves efficiency, high autonomy increases errors.



\## System Overview

\- Perception: cameras + sensors + pretrained models

\- Reasoning: rule-based + AI-assisted

\- Action: device control via Home Assistant

\- Compute: hybrid edge + central



\## Experiment Plan

Compare:

\- Low autonomy

\- Medium autonomy

\- High autonomy



Metrics:

\- success rate

\- overrides

\- critical failures



\## Key Constraint

Focus on evaluation, not feature expansion
