an empirical operationalization of autonomy calibration in smart-home AI.


Prior qualitative work identified autonomy–agency tension as a central challenge in smart-home AI expectations management, but lacked quantitative evaluation of how varying autonomy levels affect reliability, intervention frequency, and operational safety.

The referenced work studies how practitioners socially and ethically manage expectations around smart-home AI, whereas my work experimentally evaluates how varying operational autonomy levels affect reliability, intervention behavior, and system performance in smart-home environments.
